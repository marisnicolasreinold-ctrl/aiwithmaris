From Excel chaos to a clean data base: the underrated AI precondition

6 August 2026 · 2 min read · Maris Reinold

Everyone talks about the AI. Almost nobody about what it stands on: your data. And in smaller firms that data often lives in fourteen Excel files, three naming logics, and the head of a colleague who's on holiday next week. That's exactly where most projects fail — not on the AI.

Why this slows the AI down

An AI can only be as good as the data it sees. If that's contradictory, duplicated or full of gaps, it guesses — convincingly, but wrong. "Garbage in, garbage out" counts double here: because AI sounds so confident, bad input only shows up late.

You don't need a data warehouse

The good news: you don't have to spend a year building a data platform first. For the first use case, a single clean table is enough. Starting small beats the grand move — with data too.

Three pragmatic steps

  • One source of truth. For each thing, exactly one place holding the valid state — not five versions in five folders.
  • Consistent naming. Name the same things the same way. Sounds trivial, but it's half the battle for AI to find connections.
  • Only tidy what the case needs. Not the whole legacy pile, just the data for this one use case. The rest can wait.

Staying honest

Ordering data is unspectacular. There's no slick demo video for it. But it's the step with the biggest leverage: skip it and AI feels like a disappointment. Do it — small and targeted — and you lay the foundation everything else can actually stand on.
